TANKS.PLAY V0.2

Battle shapes, upgrade stats, unlock classes, and outplay bots.

How to Play

Desktop: WASD to move. Mouse to aim and shoot.
Mobile/Tablet: Left stick moves. Right stick aims and fires.

Destroy all shapes (Squares, Triangles, Pentagons, and rarer polygons) to earn Score + Coins.
Ramming shapes damages them heavily, but also hurts your tank.
Bigger shapes are rarer and give better rewards. Star shapes are the rarest and toughest.
Use the left arrow to open the Upgrade Menu and spend coins on stats.
Use Change Class (top-right) to buy stronger tanks with unique weapons.
Difficulty settings change bot strength and overall pressure.
Use Pause or Home at the top at any time.
